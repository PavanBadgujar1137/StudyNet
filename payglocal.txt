Based on your active PayGlocal dashboard, follow these exact steps to find your credentials:

---

### Step 1: Click on **"Integration"** in your Sidebar
In the left menu shown in your screenshot:
1. Click on **`Integration`** (the 3rd item with the plug icon 🔌).
*(If you ever don't see it there, click on **`Configure` ▾** → **API / Developer Settings**).*

---

### Step 2: Copy your 3 Keys
On the **Integration** page, you will see your API credentials:

1. **Merchant ID**:
   - Copy the value shown under **Merchant ID / MID**.
   - Paste it into:
     ```properties
     PAYGLOCAL_MERCHANT_ID=<paste_here>
     REACT_APP_PAYGLOCAL_MERCHANT_ID=<paste_here>
     ```

2. **Key ID**:
   - Copy the value under **Key ID / kid**.
   - Paste it into:
     ```properties
     PAYGLOCAL_KEY_ID=<paste_here>
     REACT_APP_PAYGLOCAL_KEY_ID=<paste_here>
     ```

3. **API Secret Key**:
   - Click **"Show"** or **"Generate Key"** next to your API key.
   - Copy the secret token and paste it into:
     ```properties
     PAYGLOCAL_API_KEY=<paste_here>
     ```

---

### Step 3: Check your Environment (UAT vs Production)
Look at the URL in your browser:

- **If the URL says `*.uat.payglocal.in`** (Test / Sandbox mode):
  ```properties
  PAYGLOCAL_ENV=uat
  PAYGLOCAL_BASE_URL=https://api.uat.payglocal.in
  ```

- **If the URL says `dashboard.payglocal.in`** (Live Production mode):
  ```properties
  PAYGLOCAL_ENV=production
  PAYGLOCAL_BASE_URL=https://api.prod.payglocal.in
  ```

---
